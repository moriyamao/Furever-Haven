1 Overview

Furever Haven ("we," "us," or "our") is a pet adoption and rescue organization based in Malolos, Bulacan, Philippines. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use our website or services.

This policy is in compliance with Republic Act No. 10173, also known as the Data Privacy Act of 2012, and its Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR), as enforced by the National Privacy Commission (NPC) of the Philippines.

By using our website and submitting any form, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy and consent to the collection and processing of your personal information as described herein.

2 Data We Collect

We only collect personal information that is necessary for the purposes described in this policy. The types of data we collect include:

  • Identity information — full name provided in adoption or service booking forms
  • Contact information — email address and phone number
  • Location information — home address provided in adoption applications
  • Message content — descriptions or messages you write in our forms
  • Lost & Found data — pet details and contact numbers submitted to our community board, stored locally on your device

We do not collect sensitive personal information such as government ID numbers, financial data, health records, or biometric data.

3 How We Use Your Data

Your personal information is used solely for the following purposes:

  • Processing your pet adoption application and sending you a confirmation email with next steps
  • Responding to service booking inquiries (veterinary, grooming, boarding, training)
  • Processing sponsorship inquiries and sending sponsor updates
  • Facilitating lost and found pet reunifications through our community board
  • Communicating with you regarding your submitted inquiry or application

We do not use your data for marketing, profiling, automated decision-making, or any purpose beyond what is listed above.

4 Legal Basis for Processing

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012, we process your personal information on the following legal bases:

  • Consent — you voluntarily submit your information through our forms
  • Contractual necessity — processing is necessary to fulfill your adoption application or service booking
  • Legitimate interests — operating a responsible animal rescue and adoption service

You may withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal.

5 Data Sharing & Third Parties

We use the following third-party service to process your data:

  • EmailJS — used to send adoption confirmation emails. Your submitted form data is transmitted through EmailJS servers to deliver your email receipt. EmailJS's own privacy policy applies to this transmission.

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with any other third party, advertiser, or data broker. We do not transfer your data outside the Philippines except as necessary for EmailJS email delivery.

Lost & Found reports are stored exclusively in your browser's localStorage. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to our servers or any third party.

6 Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purpose for which it was collected:

  • Adoption applications — retained for the duration of the adoption process and up to 1 year after for follow-up purposes
  • Service inquiries — retained for up to 6 months after the last communication
  • Lost & Found data — stored locally on your device only; you may delete it at any time through your browser settings

After the retention period, personal data is securely deleted or anonymized.

7 Your Rights Under the DPA

Under Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012), you have the following rights as a data subject:

  • Right to be informed — to know how your data is collected and used
  • Right to access — to request a copy of your personal data we hold
  • Right to rectification — to correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Right to erasure — to request deletion of your personal data
  • Right to object — to object to the processing of your personal data
  • Right to data portability — to receive your data in a usable format
  • Right to lodge a complaint — to file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (NPC) at privacy.gov.ph

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 12.

8 Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse:

  • All form inputs are sanitized before processing to prevent script injection
  • Anti-bot measures (honeypot fields and rate limiting) are in place on all forms
  • No sensitive credentials or API secrets are exposed in our website's frontend code
  • Our website is served over HTTPS

While we take reasonable precautions, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. In the event of a data breach that affects your rights and freedoms, we will notify you and the NPC as required by law.

9 Cookies & Local Storage

Our website does not use tracking cookies or advertising cookies. We use browser localStorage only for the following functional purposes:

  • Storing Lost & Found pet reports you submit (on your device only)
  • Tracking form submission timestamps for rate limiting (to prevent spam)

This data is stored entirely on your device and is never transmitted to us. You can clear it at any time through your browser's developer tools or settings.

10 Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed at children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has submitted personal information through our website, please contact us immediately and we will delete it.

11 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal obligations. When we do, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Continued use of our website after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
