❓ FAQs

Common Questions

Everything you need to know about our services.

Do I need to book in advance?
Yes, we recommend booking at least 2–3 days in advance to secure your preferred schedule. For boarding, 1 week advance notice is ideal especially during holidays.
Are your groomers and trainers certified?
Our groomers and trainers have hands-on experience with various breeds and temperaments. We use only positive, humane techniques — no punishment-based methods ever.
What areas do you serve?
We currently serve Malolos, Bulacan and Caloocan areas. Contact us to check if your location is covered for home visits or pickup arrangements.
How do I pay for services?
We accept cash and GCash. Payment is made on the day of service. For boarding, a 50% deposit is required upon booking to hold your slot.
What if my pet has a medical condition?
Please let us know about any existing conditions when booking. Our vet partner can assess if it's safe to proceed, and we'll adjust the service plan accordingly.
